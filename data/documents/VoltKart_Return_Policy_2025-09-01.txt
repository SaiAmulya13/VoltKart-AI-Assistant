VOLTKART RETURN & REFUND POLICY
===============================
Document Date: 2025-09-01
Version: 3.0
Owner: Customer Experience Team
Supersedes: Version 2.0

1. Return Window
----------------
Eligible products may be returned within the following periods from the date of delivery:
- Silver members: 15 days
- Gold members: 30 days
- Platinum members: 45 days
Exception: Smartphones may be returned within 7 days of delivery for all membership tiers.

2. Restocking Fee
-----------------
A restocking fee of 10% of the invoice value applies to opened laptops, televisions and smartphones returned for non-defect reasons. The restocking fee is waived for Platinum members.

3. Refund Timeline
------------------
Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method within 5 business days.

4. Exchanges
------------
Each order is entitled to one free exchange within the return window.
