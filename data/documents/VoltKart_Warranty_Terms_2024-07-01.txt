VOLTCARE EXTENDED WARRANTY TERMS
================================
Effective Date: 1 July 2024
Version: 2024.1

Coverage
--------
VoltCare extends the manufacturer warranty by 1 year. Eligible categories: Smartphones, Laptops and Televisions.

Price
-----
VoltCare costs 8% of the product invoice value.

Purchase Window
---------------
VoltCare must be purchased within 30 days of the product purchase date.

Claims
------
Claims are raised at any VoltKart store. A maximum of 2 claims per product per year is allowed.
